Who we are

Practical support for government procurement.

GemEasy is a brand of TradeBot Ventures Private Limited, based in Patna and serving businesses across India. Our team brings more than a decade of government procurement experience to GeM OEM vendor assessment, tender bidding and business compliance.

We help manufacturers review evidence of production capacity and quality controls. We help resellers check authorisation requirements, technical specifications and bid documents. Manufacturers seeking private-sector buyers can also work with us on performance marketing and B2B enquiry generation.

How we work

From requirements to a clear next step.

Tell us what you make or sell and share the tender or assessment you’re considering. We’ll help you identify what needs attention first.

Check eligibility

We review the product category, relevant tender conditions and available documents to flag early gaps.

Prepare the evidence

We help organise assessment and bid documents and coordinate testing, certification or CA support where relevant.

Coordinate next steps

We help you track submissions and respond to document requests or clarifications. Approvals, certifications and awards are decided by the relevant authority or certification body.
