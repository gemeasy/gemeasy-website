Our services

Procurement and marketing services.

GeM OEM Vendor Assessment

Prepare your company and factory documents for the OEM assessment on the Government e-Marketplace. We review evidence of production capacity and quality controls, flag gaps and help you prepare for desktop and video assessment.

Government Tender Bidding

Review eligibility, technical specifications and required documents before committing to a GeM, central, state or PSU tender. We help prepare your technical response, financial bid and submission checklist.
